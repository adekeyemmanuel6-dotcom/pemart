# Wear The Word — assets and content handoff

The new composition uses clearly labelled campaign and collection placeholders where approved photography is missing. These are not product photographs. Existing product images and design mockups are retained. The admin-uploaded logo remains the primary logo asset; no substitute bubble logo has been invented.

## Required imagery

- Hero: Nigerian/African adult male and female models wearing actual Wear The Word garments; editorial streetwear styling, warm daylight, architectural or neutral background, black/cream palette. Deliver 2000×2200 portrait and a landscape alternate, WebP or AVIF, ideally under 400 KB. Keep faces and print within the central safe area for responsive crops. Obtain photographer/model permissions.
- Five collections: identity, declaration, scripture, together, gifts. Supply 1200×1500 portraits and 1800×1100 alternates with consistent lighting; no text baked into photographs.
- Products: actual front, back, print detail and on-model/fit photos, approximately 1600 px on the long edge. Each image needs descriptive alt text. Confirm fabric composition, GSM, fit, packed weight and measurements before publishing.
- Gift packages: overhead closed box and complete contents layout, plus packing detail. Confirm each existing bundle's contents, price, availability, age/size requirements and gifting options in the admin. Do not create dummy packages to fill the page.
- Community: only customer photos shared with consent and genuine approved reviews. No substitute testimonials or review counts.
- Logo: original transparent SVG or PNG, stacked and horizontal versions if available. Upload the original in Settings. Current fallback is a plain typographic identifier, not a redesign of the bubble logo.

## Admin workflow

After reviewed migration 0014 is applied, Settings manages the hero image, alt text, headline, supporting copy, announcement, dispatch origin and free-delivery threshold. Catalogue manages collections and banners, products, prices, inventory and gift bundle slots. Products can have multiple comma-separated themes and brand collection slugs, alongside their original collection ID. Existing URLs and order relationships remain intact.

Confirm existing brand collection assignments and add missing assignments using real product IDs. An empty collection shows an honest empty state rather than invented inventory. The initial migration assigns known scripture and matching designs; it does not create the sample products listed in the brief.

## Before release

Confirm all shipping rates with the carrier, including city coverage, service, included packed weight and fees. Existing rates default to unverified and cannot be charged until checked. Confirm production times, returns terms, size guide, actual WhatsApp/social links, brand/legal ownership, and every Scripture quotation and translation in stored product content. Existing product copy is not evidence of a verified textile specification or Bible translation.

The homepage uses “Pieces with purpose” for editorially featured products. A best-seller ranking requires actual sales data; it is not inferred from the featured flag.
